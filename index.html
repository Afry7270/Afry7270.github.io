About Me

Connecting robotics software, electronics, and the physical world.

I'm a Mechatronics Engineer interested in designing and developing robotic systems that can sense, understand, and interact with their environments. My main areas of focus are robot operating systems, autonomous navigation, computer vision, automation, and embedded control.

I approach projects from a systems perspective: integrating sensors, processing data, developing control logic, and testing the complete system in realistic conditions.
